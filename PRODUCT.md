# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Small-business owners in Colombia deciding whether to commission a website. They open these demos from a services page, usually on a phone, to judge what their own site could look like.
- Inside the fiction: diners looking for a table.

## Product Purpose

A demo landing page that shows a complete site for one kind of local business:

- A restaurant: menu, opening hours, location, and booking a table.

Success: an owner of a similar business recognises their own business in the demo and asks for a quote.

## Positioning

The page belongs to one concrete business in one place, and could not be reused for another business by swapping the logo.

## Operating Context

Spanish-language audience in Colombia, prices in Colombian pesos, mobile first, WhatsApp as the usual contact channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack stays: Vue 3, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS v4. No backend.
- One repository and one deployment per demo.
- The business does not exist. Contact actions must not message a real third party.
- Photos come from free-licence libraries (Unsplash or Pexels). The list is approved by the owner before any download.
- Invented name: La Marea.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no real customers, testimonials, reviews, awards, certifications, or years in business, and none may be invented. Prices, menus, names, and schedules are illustrative and must be labelled as a demo.

## Product Principles

- One business, one place, one next step.
- Useful facts (what, how much, when, where) before persuasion.
- Fiction is always labelled as a demo.
